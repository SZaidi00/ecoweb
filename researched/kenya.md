# Region: Kenya — multi-biome country sweep
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — awaiting independent verification)

**Scope:** Kenya's Rift Valley and other inland lakes, rivers and wetlands, savanna/forest/soil systems, mangroves and estuaries, and coastal models explicitly bounded to Kenya. The country was treated as one target, consistent with the earlier Thailand and Malaysia country sweeps. The already-recorded `lake-turkana` candidate remains owned by `implemented/researched/africa-research.md` and `implemented/verified/africa.md`; it was not re-scored or duplicated. The Kenyan portion of Lake Victoria remains covered by the earlier `lake-victoria` record. Kenya subsets of the already-verified `global-reef-fish-webs` deposit and the broader `caffee-wio-reef` model likewise were not repackaged as new candidates.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live; no implemented Kenya pin. Biome saturation: marine 6, freshwater 3 and grassland 3 (all saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1. A mangrove–seagrass coastal bay can therefore pass as an estuary only if Stage 2 confirms that classification; otherwise the marine-saturation gate applies. All freshwater candidates are appendix-only under the current rule regardless of their other merits.

**Verdict:** buildable — one Tier 1 candidate, `gazi-bay`, with 23 natural source groups and a documented weighted diet-matrix location. No Tier 2 candidate is recommended. Five records are appendix-only: Nyali–Mombasa is blocked by marine saturation; Ungwana Bay currently has only an MSc thesis as the model source; Lake Baringo is too coarse and freshwater is saturated; Naivasha and Nakuru have unretrieved matrices, uncertain aggregation/richness, and the same freshwater gate.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` means a citable source and plausible matrix location were found, but the matrix was not independently obtained, reconciled and transcribed for Stage 2. A table inspected only at discovery level remains `ASSUMED`. `INACCESSIBLE` means the matrix could not be obtained in this sweep. Neither label is Stage-2 verification.
