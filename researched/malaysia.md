# Region: Malaysia — terrestrial, freshwater, estuarine & surrounding marine waters
**Researched:** 2026-11-22 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — awaiting independent verification)

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live; Malaysia has no implemented pin and no previous Malaysia ledger row. An earlier broad Asia sweep noted the Sabah/Sarawak EcoBase registration but did not candidate it. Biome saturation: marine 6 (saturated), freshwater 3 (saturated), grassland 3 (saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1, tropical terrestrial 0. Scope: Peninsular Malaysia and Malaysian Borneo (Sabah and Sarawak), including phytotelmata, forests, rivers and rice fields, mangroves and other estuaries, and coastal models explicitly bounded to Malaysia. The country was treated as one target, consistent with the earlier Pakistan and Japan country sweeps, not split into continent-scale subregions. Out of scope: Brunei, Indonesian Borneo/Kalimantan, Singapore, the already-completed Sumatra rainforest candidate, and non-trophic interaction networks.

**Outcome:** two genuine qualitative phytotelm webs from Beaver (1985), mirrored in full by Mangal and Netzschleuder, pass every current hard gate at discovery level. They are alternatives for one Malaysian pitcher-plant exhibit, not two near-duplicate pins to build automatically. *Nepenthes albomarginata* has 20 nodes/44 links; *N. ampullaria* has 19 nodes/55 links. Four additional candidates are appendix-only: three strong coastal Ecopath models are blocked by marine saturation (Terengganu also fails richness), while Matang's otherwise excellent estuarine evidence lacks a complete community matrix. No Tier 2 candidate is recommended.

## Rejected in sweep

- **Lambir Hills rainforest isotope web, Sarawak** — Hyodo, F., Matsumoto, T., Takematsu, Y., Kamoi, T., Fukuda, D., Nakagawa, M. & Itioka, T. (2010). The structure of a food web in a tropical rain forest in Malaysia based on carbon and nitrogen stable isotope ratios. *Journal of Tropical Ecology* 26:205–214. [DOI 10.1017/S0266467409990502](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266467409990502). Valuable above-/below-ground isotope evidence across four trophic groups, but no pairwise community adjacency or diet matrix was located. Isotope positions cannot be converted into invented feeding links.
- **Sabah bird–mammal ecosystem energetics** — Malhi, Y. et al. (2022). Logged tropical forests have amplified and diverse ecosystem energetics. *Nature* 612:707–713. [DOI 10.1038/s41586-022-05523-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05523-1). The open study covers 104 mammal and 144 bird species and broad plant/resource pathways, but diets are fractional food-type assignments by expert judgment and energy expenditure is allometric. It is not an observed species-to-species community food web and cannot be curated as one.
- **Setiu Lagoon, Sungai Pulai/Johor Strait and Kelantan Delta isotope webs** — Setiu's Le et al. (2017), [DOI 10.1016/j.rsma.2016.12.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rsma.2016.12.013), quantifies primary-source contributions to selected invertebrates and four fishes; Johor/Kelantan results similarly provide isotope signatures and preliminary trophic diagrams. None supplied a complete consumer-to-consumer matrix. Their estuarine setting is attractive, but the edge-data gate fails.
- **Perak rivers and rice fields** — Zainordin, A.F. et al. (2017). Determination of Trophic Structure in Selected Freshwater Ecosystems by using Stable Isotope Analysis. *Tropical Life Sciences Research* 28(2). [Open article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5584840/). The article infers four broad levels (plants → aquatic insects → invertebrate predators → fish) from isotope plots and feeding guilds, not an auditable pairwise matrix. Freshwater is also saturated.
- **Mulu phytotelmata and Peninsular bamboo internodes** — the Mulu material located is a conference abstract, and the Kovac & Streit bamboo chapter is paywalled with no matrix obtained. Neither displaces the accessible Beaver networks or meets the current discovery-level evidence standard.
- **Out-of-country Borneo webs** — Clarke & Kitching's six *Nepenthes* webs were sampled in Brunei; Yule, Boyero & Marchant's Kelian River webs are in Indonesian Borneo. Both are outside the Malaysia target and should be considered only in their own regional sweeps.
- **Host-specific or non-food-web networks** — Mangal's Malaysian bird–fruit interactions, fig/pollination networks, fungus–fungivore associations and mammal-dung/dung-beetle networks are genuine interaction data but are outside the current whole-food-web product surface or lack a multilevel trophic structure.
- **Singapore tropical reservoir webs** — Wilkinson et al. (2022) provide empirical matrices but are outside Malaysia. They are not evidence for a Malaysian reservoir web and were not promoted.

## Recommendation

Verify `nepenthes-albomarginata-web` first. Stage 2 should download Mangal network 1449's full nodes/interactions, compare every name and link with Beaver (1985) and the open Luczkovich reproduction, resolve bacteria/protozoa and taxonomy discrepancies, and confirm the original study location.
