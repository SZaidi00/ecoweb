# Region: Argentina — northern and central systems (Patagonia excluded)
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — awaiting verification)

**Scope:** Northern and central Argentina, including the Chaco Serrano, Monte, Pampas, Yungas, Iberá, the middle and lower Paraná, Río de la Plata, Mar Chiquita and Bahía Blanca. Argentine and Chilean Patagonia are excluded because they were covered by the completed Patagonia sweep (`implemented/researched/patagonia.md`). Existing Patagonian records (`san-jorge-gulf`, `golfo-nuevo-green-crab`, `lake-moreno`, `beagle-channel`, `strait-of-magellan` and `chilean-patagonia-ewe`) were not re-scored or duplicated. The already-logged Brazilian `upper-parana-floodplain` is also distinct from this file's middle Paraná candidate.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live; South America has implemented pins in Brazil and Chile. Biome saturation: marine 6 (saturated), freshwater 3 (saturated), grassland 3 (saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1. Estuarine, riverine-wetland and dry-forest candidates therefore received the strongest geography scores. The middle Paraná is treated as a riverine wetland because the source explicitly frames it as a subtropical wetland and river-floodplain system, not relabeled merely to bypass the freshwater gate.

**Outcome:** three Tier 1 candidates. Río de la Plata offers a weighted 37-group estuary–shelf model with an open diet-matrix supplement. The middle Paraná model offers 21 consumer taxa plus three basal resources, within the node window without further aggregation. Chaco Serrano offers replicated empirical plant–leaf-miner–parasitoid matrices on Dryad, but requires careful selection and aggregation. Bahía Blanca's microbial web fails richness/scope and Mar Chiquita's isotope study does not supply pairwise feeding links. No Tier 2 candidate was assigned.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` means a citable source and a concrete matrix location were found, but the matrix was not independently obtained and checked in Stage 1. `INACCESSIBLE` means the required edge/diet matrix could not be obtained in this sweep. Neither label is Stage-2 verification.

## Rejected in sweep

### Monte Desert terrestrial food web — `monte-terrestrial-web`
- **Source lead:** Bertiller, M.B., Marone, L., Baldi, R. & Ares, J.O. (2009). Biological interactions at different spatial scales in the Monte desert of Argentina. *Journal of Arid Environments* 73(2):212–221. [DOI 10.1016/j.jaridenv.2007.08.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaridenv.2007.08.008).
- **Why rejected:** the review covers competition, facilitation, plant–soil–microbe and animal interactions, while the best-resolved Villavicencio networks are plant–pollinator mutualisms. No resolved whole-community trophic edge matrix was found. Species-specific diets and interaction reviews cannot be turned into an empirical web without a separately scoped composite study.

### Iberá wetland food web — `ibera-wetland-web`
- **Source lead:** Soneira, P.A., Ruiz Díaz, F.J., Casciotta, J.R. & Almirón, A.E. (2016). Diet of *Hyphessobrycon auca* (Pisces, Characidae) in Iberá Wetland (Northeastern, Argentina). *FACENA* 27:3–8. [DOI 10.30972/fac.270923](https://doi.org/10.30972/fac.270923).
- **Why rejected:** searches found single-species diets and general biodiversity material, not a resolved community matrix. Iberá's jaguar/caiman rewilding story would be valuable, but it cannot justify reconstructing an unsupported web.
