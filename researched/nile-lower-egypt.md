# Region: Nile River — Lower Nile and Nile Delta, Egypt
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — awaiting verification)

**Scope:** The requested whole-Nile target is much broader than the implemented webs, so this sweep is limited to Egypt's Lower Nile and Nile Delta: Lake Nasser, the main Nile downstream of Aswan, the Rosetta and Damietta branches, and the Delta's coastal lakes/lagoons. It does not cover Lake Victoria, the White or Blue Nile headwaters, the Sudd, or the open Mediterranean. The previously archived `lake-victoria` candidate remains owned by the Africa sweep and was not re-scored or duplicated.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live; no implemented Nile or Egyptian pin. Freshwater is saturated with 3 live webs, estuary has 2, and wetland has 1. A coastal Delta lagoon/wetland therefore has a route through the geography gate; a new reservoir or river-freshwater web would be appendix-only under the current saturation rule.

**Verdict:** buildable — one Tier 1 candidate, `lake-burullus`. Its open supplement contains a 16-compartment qualitative interaction matrix, so it fits the node ceiling without aggregation. No Tier 2 or appendix candidate was retained. The other Nile sources found were partial fish-diet studies, plankton/biogeochemical models, contaminant studies, or unresolved taxon records rather than community food webs.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` is a Stage-1 accessibility label, not independent verification. Here the supplement and matrix were obtained, but the main article and its full group definitions were not; Stage 2 must check both. `INACCESSIBLE` means no usable matrix was obtained.

## Rejected in sweep

- **Lake Nasser nutrient/phytoplankton model** — Darwish, M. & Younis, M. (1982), *Lake Ecosystem Modelling: A Case Study of Lake Nasser in Egypt*, IFAC Proceedings Volumes, pp. 533–539, [PII S1474667017634013](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1474667017634013). The abstract describes two models of phytoplankton and nutrients for water quality, not a resolved consumer food web. No separate Lake Nasser community matrix was found; freshwater is also saturated.
- **El-Qanater Al-Khairia Nile fish diets** — El-Naggar, H.A., Khalaf Allah, H.M.M., Masood, M.F., Shaban, W.M. & Bashar, M.A.E. (2019), *Egyptian Journal of Aquatic Research* 45(3):273–280, [DOI 10.1016/j.ejar.2019.08.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejar.2019.08.004). Open field study of four fish species plus plankton/benthos availability and selectivity; valuable diet evidence, but a predator subset is not a whole-community web.
- **Damietta and Rosetta branch diet/assemblage studies** — searches found three-cichlid stomach-content work, zooplankton/ciliate surveys and estuarine reviews. None supplied a community edge matrix; do not connect species inventories or isolated fish diets into an invented web.
- **Lake Manzala, Lake Maryut and Lake Edku** — aquaculture, restoration, water-quality, biodiversity and contaminant publications surfaced, but no resolved community food-web model or matrix. Lake Maryut's cage-aquaculture dispersion study is not a food web.
- **Lake Burullus heavy-metal and macrophyte studies** — metal measurements across phytoplankton, zooplankton and Nile tilapia demonstrate contaminant pathways, not a full link matrix; Eid et al. (2012), [DOI 10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2012.05.028](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2012.05.028), is a single-macrophyte growth model. Neither is an additional web candidate.
- **Raw GloBI/Mangal-style taxon interactions and repository deposits** — isolated Nile taxon records, parasite records, fish nutrition and taxonomy deposits are not coherent published Lower Nile community webs.

## Verification priorities

1. **Obtain the Bocci et al. main article** through library/author access and reconcile its compartment Table 2 with supplement Table S5; confirm every abbreviation, definition, aggregation statement and matrix orientation.
2. **Check model provenance:** identify which links and parameters are local observations versus literature or balancing assumptions. Do not call the web measured merely because the model uses field data.
3. **Select one published realization:** western, central or eastern basin, and either no-aquaculture or aquaculture scenario. Do not average basins or scenarios into a synthetic web. Prefer the reference/no-aquaculture realization only if the main text supports it as the baseline.
4. **Keep data types honest:** Table S5 supports qualitative links. Tables S6–S7 quantify compartment inputs and aggregate flows, not individual diet shares; do not convert them into edge weights without a documented per-link flow matrix.
5. **Verify the cascade framing:** aquaculture effects are scenario results. Separately verify any claims about fishing, bird loss, eutrophication or observed ecological change before using them in UI copy.

## Recommendation

Advance only `lake-burullus` to independent Stage 2 verification. It is the sole candidate with a published, obtainable, naturally sized community matrix. If the main article cannot be obtained or its group definitions cannot be reconciled with Table S5, park the candidate rather than reconstructing names or links from abbreviations.
