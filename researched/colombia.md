# Region: Colombia — terrestrial, freshwater, wetland, estuarine & surrounding marine waters
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — awaiting independent verification)

**Scope:** Colombia was treated as one country-scale target, consistent with earlier country sweeps. Mainland Andean, Caribbean, Pacific, Orinoquian and Amazonian systems were included, together with Malpelo Island and the country's Caribbean and Pacific waters. Non-trophic mutualisms, host–parasitoid-only networks, paleo-food-webs and single-species diet studies without a community matrix were excluded. Existing South America, coral-reef, deep-sea and tropical-terrestrial records were not re-scored or duplicated.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 15 live webs; Colombia has no implemented pin and no previous ledger row. Biome saturation: marine 6, freshwater 3 and grassland 3 (all saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1, tropical terrestrial 0. A bounded estuary or tropical terrestrial web therefore has the clearest route through the gates. Freshwater and marine candidates are retained for the record but not advanced under the current saturation rule.

**Outcome:** ten retained candidates — one Tier 1, one Tier 2 and eight appendix. Bahía Málaga is the clear first target: a peer-reviewed, weighted, 18-group mangrove Ecopath model that fits the node window without aggregation. Malpelo Island's terrestrial web fills the empty tropical-terrestrial biome and has a strong marine-subsidy story, but its 27-group/98-link matrix is not deposited as a clean edge list, so it is a Tier 2 access-dependent backup. San Pedro–Pichidó has an unusually useful open thesis with a printed 26-group diet matrix, but thesis-only provenance keeps it out of the build tiers. The wetland, freshwater and marine alternatives are blocked by matrix access, aggregation cost or biome saturation. All accessibility labels are Stage 1 assessments, not independent verification.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` means a citable source and a plausible matrix route were found, but the complete matrix was not independently transcribed and checked. `INACCESSIBLE` means the complete source-resolved matrix was not obtained in this sweep. Neither label is Stage-2 verification. No candidate is marked VERIFIED.

## Rejected in sweep

- **Riverine mangrove stable-isotope web, central Colombian Pacific** — Medina Contreras, D., Cantera Kintz, J., Sánchez González, A. & Mancera, E. (2018). Food Web Structure and Trophic Relations in a Riverine Mangrove System of the Tropical Eastern Pacific, Central Coast of Colombia. *Estuaries and Coasts* 41:1511–1521. [DOI 10.1007/s12237-017-0350-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12237-017-0350-y). Four trophic levels and SIAR basal-source estimates are published, but no complete consumer–resource adjacency matrix was located. Isotope biplots cannot substitute for feeding links.
- **Malpelo marine food web** — the doctoral thesis reports 143 trophogroups/838 links; a newer article reports 143 nodes. It fails marine saturation and requires extreme aggregation, so it was not duplicated as another appendix candidate alongside the terrestrial web. Do not combine the terrestrial and marine matrices to bypass the terrestrial candidate's access problem.
- **Paja Formation paleo-network** — Cortés, D. & Larsson, H.C.E. (2024), *Zoological Journal of the Linnean Society* 202:1–19, [DOI 10.1093/zoolinnean/zlad092](https://doi.org/10.1093/zoolinnean/zlad092). A model-based Early Cretaceous reconstruction, not a modern empirical ecosystem web; its restricted Zenodo model record and open figures do not change that scope rejection.
- **Ciénaga Grande de Santa Marta species-diet and microplastic studies** — searches found single-species stomach contents, a literature-based feeding-habit table and microplastic exposure work, not a resolved community matrix. The 2022 Ecotracer proof-of-concept article is about Maryland Coastal Bays, not Ciénaga Grande, and was not misassigned to Colombia.
- **Andean/Orinoquian stream and reservoir studies** — macroinvertebrate functional feeding groups, isotope trophic positions, plankton simulations and species-specific diets lack a complete community edge matrix; freshwater is also saturated.

- **Mutualistic and host-only networks** — Colombian bird–fruit, pollination and host–parasitoid datasets are outside the current whole-food-web product surface and were not padded into trophic webs.

## Verification priorities

1. **Verify `bahia-malaga` first.** Obtain the complete 2017 article/model, confirm the 18 group names and the full diet matrix, and check diet-column sums and provenance for every pooled group. Do not use the nearby San Pedro thesis to fill missing Bahía Málaga links.
2. **Verify `malpelo-terrestrial` only if a complete matrix can be obtained.** Request the 27×27 binary matrix from the authors or establish that Fig. 10A is unambiguous enough for independent transcription. Check all 98 links against the thesis's diet references and explicitly document the local-versus-similar-species evidence. Two merges must be designed before implementation.
3. **Use San Pedro–Pichidó as a publication-watch lead, not a workaround.** Its Table 3 appears to be the best open diet matrix in the sweep, but thesis-only provenance is decisive. Reassess if the model becomes a peer-reviewed publication or formal agency model report.
4. **For Zapatosa/Ayapel, retrieve the underlying seasonal model memorias.** Select one dated hydrological period, obtain its complete matrix and group key, and demonstrate the 40/56 → ≤25 aggregation before reconsidering either as a wetland build.
5. **Leave the remaining candidates record-only.** Yahuarcaca needs both a freshwater-policy change and a complete seasonal matrix; the anuran webs lack whole-community depth; Salamanca, La Guajira and Tortugas remain blocked by marine saturation regardless of their matrix quality.

## Recommendation

Advance `bahia-malaga` as the sole first verification target. It has the right raw size, weighted model structure, distinctive high-rainfall mangrove narrative and no aggregation requirement. `malpelo-terrestrial` is the strategic backup for the empty tropical-terrestrial biome, but only after the complete 98-link matrix and composite provenance are independently resolved. The San Pedro thesis, Magdalena–Cauca wetland models and open marine matrices are valuable records, not substitutes for the required publication, access and saturation gates.
