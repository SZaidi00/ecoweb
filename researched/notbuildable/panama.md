# Region: Panama — terrestrial, freshwater, estuarine & surrounding marine waters
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — NOT BUILDABLE under current gates; parked)

**Scope:** Panama as one country-scale target: lowland and montane forests, arboreal microhabitats and soils; lakes, reservoirs and streams; mangroves, estuaries and wetlands; Pacific and Caribbean coastal waters including Taboguilla and Coiba. The country was treated as one target, consistent with earlier country sweeps, not as a continent-scale region. Out of scope: Colombia's Panama Bight, Costa Rica, non-trophic mutualistic networks, host–parasitoid-only networks, and classroom food-web exercises. The existing `bci-seed-predators` Tier 2 row in `researched/tropical-terrestrial.md` was not re-swept, moved, or duplicated. The pan-tropical `team-tropical-mammal-webs` candidate was likewise left in its original file.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live; no implemented Panamanian pin. Biome saturation: marine 6 (saturated), freshwater 3 (saturated), grassland 3 (saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1, tropical terrestrial 0. A resolved terrestrial web was therefore the only plausible unsaturated route in this sweep.

**Verdict:** NOT BUILDABLE — no new Tier 1 or Tier 2 candidate. The best unsaturated-biome lead, the *Attalea butyracea* palm-crown web study, describes 49 binary feeding matrices but its public workbook contains community data and derived metrics, not those matrices. Two classic resolved community webs, pre-invasion Gatun Lake (17 nodes/32 links) and Taboguilla Island (28 nodes/69 links), are blocked by freshwater and marine saturation respectively. Río Guabal's quantitative stream web is also freshwater-blocked. Coiba's new 42-group Ecopath model is marine-blocked and its diet matrix was not obtained. The pre-existing BCI seed-predator Tier 2 candidate remains unchanged in the separate tropical-terrestrial queue and does not alter this country-specific sweep's verdict.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` means a citable source and concrete matrix location were found but the matrix was not independently checked or transcribed in Stage 1. `INACCESSIBLE` means the required node/edge data were not obtained in this sweep. Neither label is Stage-2 verification.

## Rejected in sweep

- **Modern Gatun/Bayano isotope food webs** — Sharpe et al. (2023), DOI 10.1002/ecy.4173, and Castellanos-Galindo et al. (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2025.01.019, characterize trophic positions, isotopic niches and invasion-driven change. They do not publish resolved predator–prey adjacency or diet matrices. Do not convert isotope biplots into feeding edges or mix modern taxa into the classic 17-node snapshot.
- **Gulf of Montijo and Bocas del Toro mangrove fish assemblages** — Stuthmann, Castellanos-Galindo & Robertson (2022), DOI 10.1111/ddi.13519, provides functional traits and stable-isotope metrics for 25 Pacific and 26 Caribbean fish species. It does not resolve pairwise diets or a whole-community matrix; figshare deposits are traits/isotopes, not food-web links.
- **Río María grazer studies** — isotope and gut-content work documents selected grazing insects and tadpoles before/after amphibian decline, but not a complete community web. Do not pad this partial module from Río Guabal's separate food webs.
- **BCI tree-hole and animalivorous-bat subwebs** — odonate–mosquito/tadpole experiments and a 15-species leaf-nosed-bat dietary guild are valuable predator/prey modules, not bounded whole-community matrices. No complete tree-hole adjacency matrix was located.
- **Soil, litter and bromeliad inventories** — studies report abundance, trophic guilds, decomposition or nematode feeding categories, not observed consumer–resource matrices. Guild assignments cannot be expanded into a web without inventing links.
- **Bahía Málaga yellow-snapper network, Colombia** — Medina-Contreras et al. (2025) built a 16-trophogroup/72-connection weighted network around *Lutjanus argentiventris*. The site is Quebrada Valencia, Bahía Málaga, Colombia. “Panama Bight” is a marine biogeographic region, not the country of Panama; the study is out of scope.
- **Mutualistic, host–parasitoid and classroom networks** — BCI seed dispersal (`M_SD_032`), plant–pollinator/frugivore records, host–parasitoid-only datasets and Darién teaching worksheets are not published whole food webs suitable for this product surface.
