# Region: Central Chile — Mediterranean, coastal and offshore systems (30–40°S)
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — NOT BUILDABLE under current gates; parked)

**Scope:** Chile was split rather than swept as one very long, multi-biome country. This file covers central Chile from approximately 30–40°S: the semi-arid and Mediterranean interior, Mocha Island, rocky shores, coastal bays and the central-Chile upwelling shelf. Northern Chile north of 30°S and Patagonia south of 40°S are excluded. The existing Patagonia records `magellanic-rabbit-steppe`, `strait-of-magellan` and `chilean-patagonia-ewe` were not re-scored or duplicated.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live. Chile already has one implemented pin, the Magellanic/Fuegian steppe. Biome saturation: marine 6 (saturated), freshwater 3 (saturated), grassland 3 (saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1. Central-Chile marine candidates are therefore appendix-only even when their numerical scores and raw group counts are excellent. Mediterranean terrestrial, island and estuarine/wetland systems offered the only plausible unsaturated routes.

**Outcome:** no Tier 1 or Tier 2 candidate under the current gates. The 21-group central-Chile upwelling model, 17-group Tongoy Bay model and 19-group San Vicente Bay model have open weighted diet matrices and need little or no aggregation, but all are marine. The two broad terrestrial networks fail matrix access: the Mediterranean paper's named GitHub deposit is absent, and the Las Chinchillas paper explicitly says its data are unavailable. Mocha Island is a species-focused isotope study rather than an obtainable whole-community edge matrix. No resolved central-Chile estuarine, wetland, lake or river community matrix was found.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` means a citable source and a concrete matrix location were found, but the matrix was not independently obtained/transcribed in Stage 1. `INACCESSIBLE` means the required edge/diet matrix could not be obtained in this sweep. Neither label is Stage-2 verification.

Food web of Mocha Island (Chile) reveals the interaction between the invasive *Rattus rattus* and the endemic anuran *Eupsophus insularis*. *Biological Invasions* 25:7–15. [DOI 10.1007/s10530-022-02905-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10530-022-02905-4).
- **Why rejected:** the study combines diets and stable isotopes to examine overlap and predation between invasive black rats and the endemic Mocha Island ground frog. It is valuable supporting evidence but not an obtained, resolved whole-island community matrix of 15–25 coherent nodes. The full article is paywalled and its data statement is “available from the corresponding author on reasonable request”; no public edge/diet matrix or raw-size basis was found. Do not convert isotope niches into feeding links.

### Other rejected or out-of-scope leads

- **Mediterranean-stream *Xenopus laevis*** — Zenodo [17607237](https://zenodo.org/records/17607237) and its associated paper concern one invader's diet, isotope position and heavy metals. They are not a resolved stream-community food-web matrix.
- **Cruces River wetland, Biobío estuary, Rapel, Laja and Lanalhue** — searches found hydrology, sediment, biodiversity, trophic-state and selected-diet studies, not a citable community edge/diet matrix. Stable-isotope biplots and contaminant transfer are not feeding matrices.
- **Mocha Island cold-seep chemosynthesis** — Sellanes et al. (2011), [DOI 10.1016/j.ecss.2011.10.016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecss.2011.10.016), traces isotopic support for selected benthos; it does not provide a resolved community edge matrix.
- **Central-Chile pollination and frugivory networks** — genuine published interactions, including Mangal's `arroyo_1982`, but outside the current trophic product surface.
- **Non-trophic Chilean intertidal links** — competition, facilitation, habitat creation and behavior layers in Kéfi et al. are outside the feeding-edge schema and were not counted as candidate trophic links.
- **Northern Chile and Patagonian models** — outside this sub-region. Existing Patagonia ledger rows remain unchanged; northern Chile should be a separate sweep if requested.

## Recommendation

Park central Chile at `researched/notbuildable/chile-central.md`; do not repeat the sweep unless the user explicitly requests it or new evidence changes a gate. This is a saturation/access verdict, not a claim that central Chile lacks excellent published food webs.

If the marine gate changes, verify the central-Chile 1992/1998 model first, then Tongoy Bay and San Vicente Bay: all three are already within the 15–25-node window and have printed weighted matrices. If the Mediterranean GitHub deposit is restored, obtain and parse its actual edge list and audit the basal-arthropod rule before reconsidering it as a high-curation-risk Tier 2 candidate. Las Chinchillas requires an author-supplied matrix; the subtidal reef requires an edge list. No candidate should be promoted by relabeling a marine bay as an estuary, reconstructing a missing matrix from figures, or converting isotope values into feeding edges.
