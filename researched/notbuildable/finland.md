# Region: Finland — terrestrial, freshwater and coastal Baltic systems
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — NOT BUILDABLE under current gates; parked)

**Scope:** Finland as one country-scale target: boreal forest and forest-floor systems, lakes, and Finnish coastal Baltic waters including Åland. The existing circumpolar `kevo-mountain-birch` appendix record was not re-swept or duplicated; its host–parasitoid scope verdict in `researched/tundra-boreal.md` remains unchanged. Whole-Baltic models already in the ledger (`baltic-sea-ecopath`) and models for Swedish, Norwegian or Russian waters without a resolved Finnish local web were excluded rather than relabeled as Finnish.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live; no implemented Finnish pin. Biome saturation: marine 6 (saturated), freshwater 3 (saturated), grassland 3 (saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1. Boreal forest remains empty, making a resolved Finnish terrestrial web the only plausible unsaturated route.

**Verdict:** NOT BUILDABLE — no Tier 1 or Tier 2 candidate. The best terrestrial lead, the *Formica aquilonia* forest-floor web, has only 14 published decomposer groups and its full interaction figure/table was not obtained. The newer North Karelia soil study supplies public data and code but generates feeding links from traits rather than empirical diets. Oulujärvi has an excellent pikeperch story but imports key lower-web links from Lake Constance. The strongest resolved local webs are marine or freshwater and therefore blocked by saturation.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` means a citable source and concrete matrix location were found but the matrix was not independently obtained/transcribed in Stage 1. `INACCESSIBLE` means the required node/edge data were not obtained in this sweep. Neither label is Stage-2 verification.

## Rejected in sweep

### Lake Oulujärvi pelagic Allometric Trophic Network — `lake-oulujarvi-atn`
- **Source:** Kokkonen, E., Kuisma, M., Hyvärinen, P., Vainikka, A., Vuorio, K., Perälä, T., Härkönen, L.S., Estlander, S. & Kuparinen, A. (2024). Effects of top predator re-establishment and fishing on a simulated food web: Allometric Trophic Network model for Lake Oulujärvi. *Ecological Modelling* 492:110715. [DOI 10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2024.110715](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2024.110715); [open PDF](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/358762448/1-s2.0-S0304380024001030-main.pdf).
- **Why rejected:** the open paper has detailed local fish stomach, cohort, plankton and fishing data and a strong pikeperch re-establishment cascade. However, it explicitly states that trophic links among zooplankton, phytoplankton, bacteria, heterotrophic nanoflagellates and autotrophic picoplankton were expected to be the same as in Lake Constance because Oulujärvi-specific links were unavailable. It also uses Lake Constance values for several initial biomasses and body-size parameters. This is a simulation model with nonlocal inferred links, not an empirical Finnish community matrix; freshwater is additionally saturated. Do not relabel the ATN output as measured diet data.

### North Karelia trait-reconstructed forest-soil webs — `north-karelia-soil-web`
- **Source:** Henneron, L., Wardle, D.A., Berg, M.P., Hättenschwiler, S., Bauhus, J., Buscot, F., et al. (2026). Tree community resource economics control soil food web multifunctionality. *Nature* 655:133–140. [DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10455-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10455-1); [open article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13322958/); [figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.31700881](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.31700881).
- **Why rejected:** the study includes North Karelia plots within its 64-plot European network and supplies excellent public data/code for 47-node local webs (six basal resources and 41 consumer guilds). But the interaction matrix is calculated from five matrices of feeding preferences, density dependence, body-mass ratios/hunting strategy, prey defense and vertical niche overlap. These are trait-generated interaction strengths, not measured Finnish feeding links. The public deposit changes access, not provenance. Promotion would require an explicit `modeled` badge decision; it cannot be an `empirical` build under current rules.

## Recommendation

Park Finland at `researched/notbuildable/finland.md`; do not repeat the sweep unless the user explicitly requests it or new evidence changes a gate. This is a richness/access/provenance and saturation verdict, not a claim that Finland lacks food-web research.

If the marine gate changes, inspect the Dryad Åland workbook first and preserve one coastal decade as a qualitative composite web. The most important terrestrial revisit would be an author- or library-supplied full Laakso & Setälä paper proving at least 15 published nodes with auditable links. North Karelia and Oulujärvi should not be promoted without a policy for modeled links; no candidate should be promoted by padding a 14-node web, importing Lake Constance links, or treating trait-generated matrices as empirical diets.
