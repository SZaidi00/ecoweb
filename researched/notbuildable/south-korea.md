# Region: South Korea — terrestrial, freshwater, estuarine & surrounding marine waters
**Researched:** 2026-11-22 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — NOT BUILDABLE under current gates; parked)

**Scope:** South Korea's mainland and Jeju Island, its freshwaters and estuaries, and coastal/offshore models explicitly covering Korean waters. North Korea is excluded. The earlier China sweep explicitly excluded Korean waters; its Yellow Sea/Bohai candidates were not re-scored. The verified transboundary `east-china-sea` model remains owned by `verified/asia-research.md` and `verified/asia.md` and was not duplicated. The country was treated as one target, not split into continent-scale subregions.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 17 webs live; no implemented South Korea pin and no previous South Korea ledger entry. Biome saturation: marine 6 (saturated), freshwater 3 (saturated), grassland 3 (saturated), estuary 2, wetland 1, desert 1. Estuarine, wetland and terrestrial candidates therefore had the clearest route through the geography gate; strong marine and freshwater models are appendix-only under the current saturation rule.

**Outcome:** South Korea has useful published aquatic food-web resources, including open weighted Ecopath matrices for Uljin marine ranch, the southwestern East Sea and Nakdong estuary, plus a national freshwater metaweb and more than 1,700 extrapolated stream webs. None is currently buildable. The raw-size-compatible marine models fail the marine-saturation gate; freshwater candidates fail saturation and sometimes richness or provenance gates; the open Nakdong, Gwangyang and Namyang models contain at most 14, 13 and 10 source groups respectively; and no resolved 15–25-node terrestrial community matrix was found. No Tier 1 or Tier 2 candidate is recommended. Numerically strong appendix candidates remain available if the gates change.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` means a citable model and a plausible matrix/link location were found, but the data were not independently transcribed and checked in Stage 1. `INACCESSIBLE` means the matrix could not be obtained in this sweep. Neither label is Stage-2 verification.

## Rejected in sweep

- **Seomjin River estuary eDNA reconstruction** — Ji, C.W., Yu, T.-S. & Kwak, I.-S. (2025), *KOFFST International Conference 2025*, p. 417, [record](https://db.koreascholar.com/Article/Detail/446337): a one-page conference abstract, not an obtainable edge matrix. eDNA co-occurrence alone is not feeding evidence.
- **Janghang/Hangang *Salix* wetland web** — Han, D., Yang, D., Lee, E.J. & Park, S. (2012), [DOI 10.1080/19768354.2011.620623](https://doi.org/10.1080/19768354.2011.620623): stable-isotope/fatty-acid evidence focused on sesarmid-crab pathways, not a resolved 15–25-node community matrix. Do not expand a crab-centered subweb into a whole estuary web.
- **Hongseong rice-field arthropod webs** — Kim, S.T., Lee, S.Y., Jung, J.-K. & Lee, J.-H. (2012), *Food Web of Main Arthropods in 3 Differently Managed Rice Fields*, conference abstract: isotope-based arthropod subwebs without an obtainable full interaction matrix; not a whole paddy community.
- **Nakdong habitat-pathway isotope studies** — Choy, E.J., An, S. & Kang, C.-K. (2008), *Estuarine, Coastal and Shelf Science* 78: 215–226: organic-matter pathways and source contributions, not a complete predator–prey matrix. The separate lower-Nakdong Ecopath models also remain freshwater/small-model records, not a substitute for the 14-group estuary model.
- **Mukho/Pohang marine-ranch isotope webs** — Kang, H.Y. et al. (2021), [DOI 10.3389/fmars.2021.653281](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2021.653281): valuable giant-octopus trophic-pathway estimates but no resolved pairwise community matrix; marine saturation is an additional gate.
- **Ulleungdo/Dokdo fish assemblages** — Park, T.H. et al. (2024), [DOI 10.3389/fmars.2024.1293542](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2024.1293542): fish-focused isotope/niche analysis, not a whole-community matrix; marine saturation applies.
- **Ulsan Bay and Geum estuary contaminant-transfer webs** — isotope-derived trophic positions and pollutant transfer are not auditable feeding matrices. Contaminants measured in taxa must not be converted into invented links.
- **Other Korean marine models** — the 2007 ICES three-ecosystem comparison, Bangjukpo, Shinjido and the 1988/89 southwestern East Sea models were not advanced because marine is saturated; the ICES record did not supply a matrix. Goesan Reservoir was not advanced because freshwater is saturated and no matrix was obtained. These are early hard-gate rejections, not claims that the models lack scientific value.
- **Terrestrial forest/soil and DMZ leads** — searches found anuran isotope niches, soil-community surveys, single-predator diets and habitat-connectivity maps, but no resolved forest, grassland, wetland or DMZ community food web. Ecological-corridor networks are not trophic networks.

## Recommendation

Park South Korea at `researched/notbuildable/south-korea.md`; do not repeat the sweep unless the user explicitly requests it or new evidence changes a gate. This is a saturation/richness/access verdict, not a claim that South Korea lacks published food webs.

If the marine gate changes, verify **Uljin first** for near-raw 19-group curation, then the **southwestern East Sea minke-whale model** for its 21-group squid/whale story. If the freshwater gate changes, inspect one site-period in the restored-stream archive before using the national KF-metaweb; both require explicit treatment of inferred links. The most important estuarine revisit would be a **new published Nakdong or Seomjin model with at least 15 source-resolved groups and a complete diet/edge matrix**. The existing 14-group Nakdong, 9–13-compartment Gwangyang and 10-group Namyang models must not be padded by splitting pooled nodes or importing unsupported links. No candidate should be promoted by relabeling a marine bay as an estuary, treating isotope values as pairwise diets, or presenting GloBI extrapolations as observed local feeding.
