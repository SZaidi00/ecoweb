# Region: Egypt — Mediterranean coastal sea
**Researched:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** complete (Stage 1 — NOT BUILDABLE; parked)

**Scope:** This extends, rather than repeats, `researched/nile-lower-egypt.md`. Egypt was split into sub-regions comparable with the implemented webs: the earlier file owns the Lower Nile and Delta; this file covers the Egyptian Mediterranean coastal sea from Marsa Matruh through Alexandria and the Nile Delta offshore to North Sinai, including Abu Qir, El-Mex, Damietta's coastal/estuarine waters and Lake Bardawil. It excludes the Red Sea, Gulfs of Suez and Aqaba, inland deserts, and the Delta lakes already swept (`lake-burullus`, Manzala, Maryut and Edku). A Lake Maruit paper encountered here was used only to cross-check the earlier negative verdict, not re-scored as a new candidate.

**Repo context at sweep time:** 16 webs live; no implemented Egyptian pin. Marine has 6 live webs and is saturated under the project's aim of no more than 3 per biome until all biomes have at least 2. Freshwater has 3, grassland 3, estuary 2, wetland 1 and desert 1. The existing `lake-burullus` Tier 1 candidate remains pending verification in the Lower Nile file and is not duplicated here.

**Verdict:** NOT BUILDABLE — no Tier 1 or Tier 2 candidate. One appendix candidate, `egyptian-mediterranean-plankton`, is recorded: an open, field-calibrated plankton process model with only four triplet pathways and at most ten living groups. It fails the 15-node minimum and marine-saturation gate. An ODYSSEA presentation says an Egyptian Ecopath model was under construction in 2019, but no published Egyptian model, functional-group list or diet matrix surfaced in the project publications, EcoBase or follow-up searches. Other sources were catch indicators, isotope budgets, plankton surveys or single-species diet studies, not resolved community food webs.

**Access terminology:** `ASSUMED` is a Stage-1 label, not independent verification. The appendix paper was obtained and inspected, but Stage 2 has not checked it. `INACCESSIBLE` means no usable node/edge data were obtained.

## Appendix — recorded, not planned

### Egyptian Mediterranean coastal plankton model — `egyptian-mediterranean-plankton`
- **Biome / Region:** Coastal marine plankton / south-eastern Mediterranean · Egypt, with nested Alexandria and Eastern Harbour simulations · **Lat/Lng (approx):** 31.20, 30.00 (Alexandria; larger model extends along the Egyptian coast)
- **Source:** Hamza, W., Ennet, P., Tamsalu, R. & Zalesny, V. (2003). The 3D physical-biological model study in the Egyptian Mediterranean coastal sea. *Aquatic Ecology* 37:307–324. [DOI 10.1023/A:1025836929341](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1025836929341). [Open PDF](https://www.vliz.be/imisdocs/publications/56662.pdf).
- **Data access:** **ASSUMED, not independently verified** — the open paper was inspected at discovery level. Figure 1 and the accompanying text define four plankton triplets; equations define feeding/growth processes. There is no community diet-percentage matrix. The prose mentions four autotroph and five heterotroph size classes, whereas the equations use `N = 4` and the four triplets plus bacterioplankton imply nine living groups. Preserve this discrepancy; either count is below the project minimum.
- **Raw size:** 9–10 living plankton groups / four triplet pathways; fish predation on mesozooplankton is explicitly treated as an external controlling factor and excluded · **Aggregation plan:** no valid plan. The model is already below 15 nodes and its size classes cannot be split into additional taxa without new sourced observations.
- **Data type:** qualitative food-web topology plus numerical process equations and local field calibration; not per-link diet fractions · **Provenance:** composite process model (1982–1986 local measurements plus literature/allometric parameterization).
- **Hard gates:** published and citable: pass · node/link definition obtainable: provisional pass pending Stage 2 · plausible 15–25-node version: **fail** · trophic structure: pass, but plankton-only · biome not saturated: **fail** (6 live marine webs).
- **Scores:** data 2 · story 2 · geography 3 · cost 1 · recognition 2 = **11**
- **Tier:** appendix · **Notes:** The Nile/sewage nutrient-loading story is valuable, and the paper compares model output with measured nutrients and plankton. It nevertheless lacks the fish, birds and other consumers needed for a satisfying whole-ecosystem cascade. Do not add excluded fish nodes, convert process coefficients into diet weights, or merge it with unrelated local diet studies to manufacture a 15-node web.

## Rejected in sweep

- **ODYSSEA Egyptian Ecopath model** — Dimarchopoulou's 2019 [training slides](https://odysseaplatform.eu/download/2nd%20ODYSSEA%20Summer%20School/Presentations/Day%205%20-%20Dimarchopoulou%20-%20Marine%20Ecosystem%20modelling.pdf) state only that models were "being built for the first time in: Morocco, Egypt and Gökova Bay." The detailed example that follows is Thermaikos Gulf, Greece, not Egypt. No Egyptian model citation, group list, matrix or subsequent publication was found; the model list and EcoBase check do not support treating this as a published candidate.
- **Nile Delta offshore fishery isotope study** — Oczkowski, A.J., Nixon, S.W., Granger, S.L., El-Sayed, A.-F.M. & McKinney, R.A. (2009). Anthropogenic enhancement of Egypt's Mediterranean fishery. *PNAS* 106:1364–1367. [Open article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2629448/). More than 600 fish and a two-endmember nitrogen mixing model support a nutrient-subsidy conclusion, but the paper does not publish predator–prey links. Isotope values and trophic-level assignments are not a community edge matrix.
- **Egyptian Mediterranean catch and EcoTroph studies** — Dimarchopoulou et al. (2021), [DOI 10.3390/w13040482](https://doi.org/10.3390/w13040482), analyses GFCM landings and mean trophic level; Khalfallah et al. (2023), *Once upon a century, the Egyptian Mediterranean fisheries (1920–2019), as affected by ‘fishing down’ and climate change*, [PII S0964569123003563](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0964569123003563), reconstructs catches. Both are useful context for a future web, but neither supplies a resolved food web.
- **Lake Maruit linkage study** — Mitwally, H. et al. (2023). Linkages between pelagic and benthic biota in a deteriorated coastal lake after restoration, Maruit, Egypt. *Environmental Monitoring and Assessment* 195. [DOI 10.1007/s10661-023-11525-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10661-023-11525-x). The open paper uses biota abundances, GLMM regressions, Bray–Curtis similarities and clusters to propose linkages. Those statistical associations are not measured trophic edges; do not transcribe its dendrogram as a food web. This confirms, rather than reopens, the Lower Nile file's negative Maryut result.
- **Damietta planktonic ciliate survey** — El-Tohamy, W.S. et al. (2024). Planktonic ciliate communities along an environmental gradient in the Nile Delta (Damietta region, Egypt). *Scientific Reports* 14. [DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-69551-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-69551-9). The study records 32 ciliate taxa at 25 sites and their environmental correlations. A taxon inventory with general background statements about ciliates grazing and being preyed upon is not a resolved local food web.
- **Bardawil, Abu Qir and El-Mex diet studies** — searches found, for example, Mokbel, S.A., Omar, A.A. & Hassanen, G.D.I. (2025), European sea-bass stomach contents from Bardawil Lagoon, [DOI 10.21608/sinjas.2025.361243.1305](https://doi.org/10.21608/sinjas.2025.361243.1305), and a three-fish diet study from El-Mex Bay. These are valuable predator-specific diet evidence but not community webs. Do not stitch separate studies into an unlabeled synthetic network.
- **Lessepsian-fish deposits and raw taxon interactions** — the [Zenodo 10412691 record](https://zenodo.org/record/10412691) compares fish from the Israeli Mediterranean and Gulf of Aqaba and contains an article PDF, not an Egyptian community matrix. Generic GloBI/Mangal-style taxon records likewise do not form a coherent published ecosystem web.
- **Regional models outside Egypt** — searches surfaced models for Israel, Cyprus, Greece, Morocco, the Gulf of Gabès and the Moroccan Mediterranean. These are out of geographic scope and were not imported as Egyptian candidates. The 31-group WASET false positive is explicitly Moroccan.

## Recommendation

Do not schedule an Egyptian Mediterranean build from this sweep. The file is parked under `researched/notbuildable/`; do not repeat it unless new evidence explicitly warrants a re-run. The strongest future trigger would be publication and deposit of the ODYSSEA Egyptian Ecopath model with its functional-group definitions and diet matrix. Even then, marine saturation must be reconsidered before promotion. Continue with the existing Lower Nile candidate `lake-burullus` through its own independent Stage 2 verification; this parked sweep has nothing to verify.
