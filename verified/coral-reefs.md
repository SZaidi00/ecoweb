## Missed-candidate pointers (not investigated beyond this line)

- **Weijerman, Fulton & Parrish (2013), PLoS ONE 8(5):e63797 (open access)** — contains *three* Ecopath mass-balance Hawaiian reef models (French Frigate Shoals, Kona/West Hawaiʻi, Oʻahu; 33 functional groups each) with diet-composition matrices stated to be in the Supporting Information (Text S1, Tables S1–S8). Agent A listed it only as a lineage pointer; it is a candidate in its own right — and would supersede the 15-node 1984 FFS model if verified.
- **McKinnon, Duggan & Logan (2003), AIMS Technical Report No. 33** — "Marine food webs of the Great Barrier Reef: a trophic synthesis of available and new data" (open AIMS PDFs); a concrete route into the GBR gap that `gbr-gribble-models` left open.
- **Dubois et al. (2019), *Fish & Fisheries*** — open supplement (`digital.csic.es/bitstream/10261/184908/4/Dubois_et_al_2019_suppl.pdf`) personally seen to contain a full "Diet matrix used in the 2006 ECOPATH model" for a Caribbean reef (which reef unconfirmed).
- **Arias-González et al. (2011), "Trophic models for investigation of fishing effect on coral reef ecosystems"** — Mexican Caribbean Ecopath models, distinct from the 1997 Tiahura paper.
- **Alvarez-Hernández (2003), "Trophic Model of a Fringing Coral Reef in the Southern Mexican Caribbean"** — open-access UBC Fisheries Centre Research Reports ("From Mexico to Brazil"), registered in the same EcoBase registry as Danajon.
- **SW-Atlantic / Brazilian reef Ecopath lineage** (Rocha et al. 2007+; Abrolhos/East-Brazil models cited in the Xisha paper's Table 2) — never swept; the Atlantic-side reef biome beyond the Caribbean.
- **Ruiz Sebastián & McClanahan (2013), W. Indian Ocean J. Mar. Sci.** — open-access CAFFEE companion paper (ajol.info) that may expose the 27-group structure without a paywall.
- **Sakka et al. (2000), MEPS 197:1–17** — a second, more finely resolved published Takapoto plankton web.
- *Not new (duplicates of existing rows/rejects, recorded to prevent double-counting): Bascompte et al. 2005 FW_008, Opitz 1996, Polovina 1984, Tiahura 1997.*
