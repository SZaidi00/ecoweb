## Verified matrix locations (for Stage 3)

- **hauraki-gulf:** balanced baseline parameters = **Appendix 5 of NZAEBR-301** (Pinkerton, Moore & O'Driscoll 2023, open PDF `webstatic.niwa.co.nz/library/NZAEBR-301.pdf`); per-group diet derivation = appendices of **AEBR 160** (Pinkerton et al. 2015, open PDF via fs.fish.govt.nz / MPI). 48 groups → ~20–24 as planned. Five-period historical family (present / ~1950s / ~1790 / ~1500 / ~1000 AD) available in AEBR 160.
- **otago-harbour-intertidal:** full node/edge tables = **Ecological Archives E092-173** (Mouritsen et al. 2011, DOI 10.1890/11-0371.1; FigShare-mirrored; GloBI-indexed). 180 nodes / 1,924 typed links (predation, parasitism, concomitant predation). 180 → ≤25 aggregation is the heavy lift, as flagged.
- **kerguelen-plateau:** 28-group balanced parameters = **Tables A1/A2 of Subramaniam et al. (2020) Front. Mar. Sci. 7:641** (open). Base-model paper = Subramaniam et al. (2020) DSR II 174:104787 (paywalled abstract; UTAS figshare record). Near-raw usability (28 groups) confirmed.
- **te-tapuwae-reef:** diet matrices = **Tables 2 (pre-MR 2008) and 3 (historical 1940s) of Eddy et al. (2014)**, open PDF (`isimip.org` mirror of Ecol. Modelling 275:48–72); original 22-group parameterisation = Pinkerton et al. (2008) JEMBE 367(2):189–203 + open DOC report (Lundquist & Pinkerton 2008, sfc288). Pick one parameterisation per Stage 1's caveat; Eddy tables are the open route.
- **east-bass-strait:** diet matrix = report Table 2 (unopened — OCR risk as flagged); **EcoBase 406** (59 groups, 11 fleets) = machine-readable backup.
- **north-west-shelf / chatham-rise / southern-plateau-nz:** matrices not opened (Tier 2 — sight at Stage 3 if promoted; NWS TR9 via WA DBCA library; Chatham model via NIWA grey literature; Southern Plateau = paywalled JEMBE paper + EcoBase).
